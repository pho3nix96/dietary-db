Food Space Map

Foods from the Swedish and Finnish food composition databases, clustered within FAO/WHO GIFT food groups by nutrient content and nutrient fractions. Food records are simulated.

Nordic Food Space MapWithin-group clusters for Sweden, Finland and both combined. Recommendation Group TreesThe nine recommendation groups split by their trees, in 3D or 2D, with servings. Food Co-intake GraphFoods eaten together in the same meal, drawn along the composition tree.
Source code and pipeline: pho3nix96/dietary-db.
